Train. Eat. Level Up.

GymProgressApp is a local-first training, nutrition and progress tracker built around personal progression rather than competition with other users.

Privacy Policy How local app data, backups and third-party services are handled. Support Release support information and current contact channel.

Current product model

The current app stores its training, nutrition, bodyweight, recovery, profile and enhanced-tracking records in a local SQLite database on the device. It does not currently provide developer-operated accounts, cloud sync, advertising or analytics.

Users can manually export a SQLite backup through the iOS share sheet and can restore a validated backup with an automatic pre-restore rollback copy.
